Getting started

What is MediZon?
An online pharmacy that also looks after your medicines once they arrive. You order from a licensed pharmacy, get a reminder when each dose is due, and MediZon tracks what's left at home so you can refill before you run out, either yourself or automatically.
Do I need an account to shop?
You can browse, search and fill a cart without one. Create a free account to check out; your cart is kept when you sign in or sign up.
How do I check that you deliver to my area?
Tap Select location at the top of any page. Use your current location, pick a saved address, enter a PIN code, search for an address or drop a pin on the map. The header then shows Delivers to or We don't deliver to for that place.
What is the MediZon Assistant?
A chat helper for signed-in users (the chat button at the bottom right). Ask it to find a medicine, add it to your cart, check today's doses or track an order. Its answers are general guidance and don't replace advice from your doctor or pharmacist.

Orders & delivery

How much is delivery?
How do I track my order?
Open Orders. Pending shows everything placed, confirmed or shipped; open an order to see each step. You also get a notification each time the status changes.
Can I cancel an order?
Yes, until it ships. Open the order from Orders and choose Cancel. Once it has shipped, you can request a return after delivery instead.
Can I send medicines to someone else's address?
Who is each medicine in my cart for?
Each cart line has a For row. Pick yourself, a family member, the household shelf for common (OTC) medicines, or Others for someone outside your family. With several packs, give each person their own count. When the order is delivered, the packs are added to that person's supply.

Prescriptions

Which medicines need a prescription?
Medicines marked Rx need a valid prescription from a doctor. Common (OTC) medicines don't.
How do I upload a prescription?
Go to Prescriptions in your account menu, or upload one at checkout. JPG, PNG or PDF up to 5 MB; the doctor's name, date and signature must be visible.
How long does approval take?
A pharmacist reviews each upload, usually within a few hours. You get a notification when it's approved or if anything needs fixing, with the pharmacist's note.
Do automatic refills of Rx medicines need a prescription?
Yes. Automatic refills of prescription medicines use your approved prescription while it's valid. Upload a new one before it expires to keep refills going.

My medicines & reminders

How do dose reminders work?
Add a medicine in My medicines with the dose and the times you take it: every day, on chosen days of the week, or on specific dates. When a dose is due you get a reminder on this site, as a desktop alert and by email.
How does MediZon know how much is left at home?
Each reminder counts the dose as taken and deducts it from what's at home. Tap Skip and it's added back. Delivered orders top up the count of the person each medicine was bought for.
I received an order. Why does it ask me to set reminders?
Delivered medicines are added to the person's supply but stay off until you add dose times and days. Open My medicines and set them from the list at the top.
What is the household shelf?
Common (OTC) medicines kept at home for anyone who needs them, like a pain reliever or antacid. They have no reminders. Add one to a person's list and what their doses need moves across.
Will I be told when a medicine is about to expire?
Yes. Each medicine shows its expiry date, and you get an alert when stock at home expires within 30 days or has expired.

Refills

What refill options are there?
For each medicine choose Alert me, I'll order, Order automatically, or No refill alerts. You also set how many days of supply should trigger it.
Where do automatic refills go?
To your default address, or to the address you picked for that medicine (for example, a parent's home). The order shows Placed by auto refill and you can cancel it until it ships.

Family

Can I manage medicines for my family?
Can each phone show only some people's reminders?
Yes. Open Alerts on this device on that phone or computer and choose everyone or only some members. Order, prescription and return updates always show. To change another device, open the page on that device.

Returns & refunds

How do I return a medicine?
What can be returned?
Damaged, wrong or expired medicines. Opened strips and medicines kept in the fridge can only be returned if they were damaged, wrong or expired on arrival.
When will I get my refund?
A pharmacist reviews each request. Once approved we pick the packs up and refund the medicines' price. Delivery charges aren't refunded. Follow progress in Orders → Returns.

Payments & invoices

How can I pay?
Cash on delivery, UPI, or a debit / credit card.
Where do I find my tax invoice?
Open the order from Orders and choose Invoice. Prices include GST and the invoice shows the tax inside each price. Print it or save it as a PDF.
What is the billing address for?
The name, address and GSTIN (for business purchases) printed on your invoices. It isn't used for delivery. Without one, orders are billed to the address they're delivered to.

Account & alerts

How do I change my password?
Open Account settings → Change password, confirm your current password, then choose a new one. You stay signed in on this device and are signed out everywhere else.
I'm not getting desktop alerts. What should I check?
Desktop alerts need your browser's permission for this site. Check the site settings in your browser and allow notifications. Dose reminder emails go to the account's email address for everyone in the family.
Can I change the language?
Yes. Choose a language from the selector at the bottom of any page. English, Hindi and other Indian languages are available.
Is MediZon's information medical advice?
No. Information on MediZon, including answers from the assistant, is general guidance and doesn't replace advice from your doctor or pharmacist. In an emergency, call 112.

Running the store

How do I process customer orders?
Open Orders, filter by status and move each order along as it's confirmed, shipped and delivered. Marking an order delivered adds its packs to the customer's supply at home.
How do returns work?
Review each request and its photos in Returns, then move approved returns through pickup and refund. Marking a return picked up also takes the packs out of the customer's supply count.
How are sales and revenue counted?
Sales are what customers paid (GST and delivery included) on orders that weren't cancelled, counted on the day the order was placed. Refunds count on the day they were paid; net revenue is sales minus refunds. The Excel export always includes a Summary sheet.
How do delivery areas work?
Customers can only order to the PIN codes in Delivery areas. Add one or many at once, separated by commas, spaces or new lines. The header's location picker checks them.
How are product prices and tax set?
Prices include GST. Invoices work out the tax inside each price from the medicine's GST rate and HSN code. Expired stock can't be ordered.
How do I add another admin?
In Users, create an admin with an email that isn't already registered. Admin accounts manage orders, prescriptions, the catalog and users; they can't shop. Customer accounts can't be turned into admins. Disabled accounts keep their orders and history.
What counts as a visit?
One browser session per day. Admin pages aren't counted.
